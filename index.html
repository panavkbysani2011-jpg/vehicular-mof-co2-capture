Mathematical Model & Governing Formulations
1. Dynamic Bed Adsorption Kinetics
$$\frac{dq(t)}{dt} = k \cdot \left[ q_{\text{eq}} - q(t) \right] \implies q(t) = q_{\text{eq}} \cdot \left(1 - e^{-k \cdot t}\right)$$
Where \(q_{\text{eq}} = m_{\text{MOF}} \cdot Q_{\text{max}} \cdot \left(\frac{C_{\text{CO}_2}}{C_{\text{ref}}}\right)\). Baseline: \(m_{\text{MOF}} = 5.0\text{ kg}\), \(Q_{\text{max}} = 0.25\text{ kg CO}_2/\text{kg MOF}\), \(k = 0.04\text{ min}^{-1}\).
2. Sigmoid Thermal Desorption Release Fraction
$$\sigma(T) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left[-k_\sigma (T - T_{\text{m}})\right]}$$
Derived from Gibbs free energy equilibrium \(\frac{\sigma}{1 - \sigma} = \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta H - T \Delta S}{R T}\right)\), giving inflection midpoint \(T_{\text{m}} = 90^\circ\text{C}\) and steepness \(k_\sigma = 0.08^\circ\text{C}^{-1}\).
3. Total Regeneration Energy Duty
$$Q_{\text{total}} = Q_{\text{sensible}} + Q_{\text{desorb}} = \left[ m_{\text{MOF}} \cdot c_{\text{MOF}} \cdot (T - T_0) \right] + \left( \frac{m_{\text{released}}}{M_{\text{CO}_2}} \cdot \Delta H_{\text{des}} \right)$$
Sensible bed warming (\(c_{\text{MOF}} = 0.8\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}\)) plus carbamate bond cleavage enthalpy (\(\Delta H_{\text{des}} = 45\text{ kJ/mol}\)).
4. Limewater Mineralization Stoichiometry & Calcite Yield
$$\text{CO}_2\text{ (aq)} + \text{Ca(OH)}_2\text{ (aq)} \longrightarrow \text{CaCO}_3\text{ (s)}\downarrow + \text{H}_2\text{O}\text{ (l)}$$ $$m_{\text{CaCO}_3} = m_{\text{released}} \cdot \left(\frac{M_{\text{CaCO}_3}}{M_{\text{CO}_2}}\right) \approx 2.274 \cdot m_{\text{released}}$$
Spontaneous calcite precipitation with negligible solubility product (\(K_{\text{sp}} = 3.3 \times 10^{-9}\)). Slurry capacity: \(n_{\text{Ca(OH)}_2} = \frac{w \cdot \rho \cdot V}{M_{\text{Ca(OH)}_2}}\).
